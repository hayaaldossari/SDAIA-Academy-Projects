Saudi Projects & Vision 2030
1. NEOM: A New Region for Innovation and Development

NEOM is a major development region in northwestern Saudi Arabia and one of the most prominent projects associated with Saudi Vision 2030. The project aims to develop new communities and economic sectors by combining technology, sustainability, innovation, and advanced infrastructure. NEOM covers a large area that includes coastal, mountainous, and desert environments along the Red Sea. Its development includes several specialized destinations, each designed around different economic and lifestyle concepts. NEOM is intended to support sectors such as tourism, technology, clean energy, advanced manufacturing, and entertainment. The project represents Saudi Arabia’s efforts to diversify its economy beyond traditional sources of income and attract international investment. It also aims to create new opportunities for businesses, researchers, entrepreneurs, and professionals in a highly connected environment.

2. The Line: A New Urban Development Concept

The Line is one of the most recognizable developments within NEOM and is designed around a new approach to urban living. The concept focuses on creating a linear city without traditional roads and cars within its main urban environment. The development is intended to prioritize walking, accessibility, technology, and efficient use of land and resources. The Line is planned to incorporate advanced infrastructure and renewable energy while reducing the environmental footprint associated with conventional cities. Its design has attracted global attention because it challenges traditional approaches to urban planning and transportation. The project is part of Saudi Arabia’s broader effort to develop future-oriented cities and introduce new models of sustainable urban development. The Line therefore represents the combination of architecture, technology, transportation, environmental planning, and economic development within Vision 2030.

3. Qiddiya: Entertainment, Sports, and Culture

Qiddiya is a major development near Riyadh focused on entertainment, sports, culture, and tourism. The project aims to create a large destination where residents and visitors can participate in recreational, sporting, cultural, and entertainment activities. Qiddiya is designed to include attractions and facilities for different age groups and interests, including sports venues, theme parks, cultural experiences, and outdoor activities. The development is part of Saudi Arabia’s efforts to expand domestic entertainment options and develop the tourism and leisure economy. Qiddiya also aims to create employment opportunities and support private-sector investment in the entertainment and sports industries. By developing a large-scale destination close to Riyadh, the project is intended to contribute to economic diversification while creating new lifestyle and leisure experiences for people living in Saudi Arabia.

4. The Red Sea Project: Luxury Tourism and Environmental Conservation

The Red Sea destination is a major tourism development on Saudi Arabia’s western coast that combines luxury hospitality with environmental conservation. The destination is being developed around islands, beaches, marine ecosystems, deserts, and other natural landscapes. A major focus of the project is creating tourism experiences while protecting sensitive coastal and marine environments. Planned activities include resorts, water-based experiences, nature exploration, and wellness tourism. The project is part of Saudi Arabia’s strategy to develop international tourism and diversify the national economy. Sustainability is an important element of the development, including efforts related to renewable energy, conservation, and responsible management of natural resources. The Red Sea destination demonstrates how Saudi Arabia is attempting to build a tourism industry based not only on cultural heritage but also on natural landscapes and environmentally conscious development.

5. Diriyah Gate: Preserving Heritage Through Modern Development

Diriyah Gate is a major development project focused on transforming the historic area of Diriyah into a global cultural and tourism destination. The project is centered around the heritage of At-Turaif, the historic district associated with the First Saudi State and recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The development aims to preserve the cultural identity and traditional Najdi architectural character of Diriyah while introducing modern hospitality, dining, retail, entertainment, and cultural experiences. Traditional architectural elements are incorporated into the design of new developments to create a connection between historical and contemporary Saudi Arabia. The project is also expected to contribute to tourism, employment, and economic activity in the Riyadh region. Diriyah Gate illustrates how historical heritage can be incorporated into large-scale urban development and used as part of Saudi Arabia’s cultural tourism strategy.

6. ROSHN: Housing and Community Development

ROSHN is a Saudi real estate developer focused on creating residential communities and improving the quality of urban living. The company is owned by the Public Investment Fund and contributes to Saudi Arabia’s housing and urban development objectives. ROSHN communities are designed to provide homes alongside services and facilities such as schools, parks, retail areas, and public spaces. The approach focuses on creating neighborhoods where residents can access important services and recreational spaces within their communities. ROSHN’s developments are located in several Saudi cities and are part of broader efforts to increase home ownership and develop modern residential environments. The project reflects Vision 2030’s focus on improving quality of life, supporting the housing sector, stimulating economic activity, and developing more integrated and livable communities.

7. New Murabba: A New Urban Center in Riyadh

New Murabba is a large-scale urban development project in Riyadh designed to create a new mixed-use district combining residential, commercial, cultural, hospitality, and entertainment functions. One of its most recognizable elements is the Mukaab, a large cube-shaped landmark intended to become a major attraction within the development. New Murabba aims to create a modern urban environment with public spaces, pedestrian-friendly areas, and advanced technology. The project is part of Riyadh’s broader transformation and supports Saudi Arabia’s goal of making the capital a major global city. It is also expected to contribute to economic diversification by attracting businesses, visitors, and investment. The development demonstrates how large-scale urban projects can combine architecture, entertainment, technology, real estate, and tourism within a single district.

8. Riyadh Metro: Transforming Urban Transportation

The Riyadh Metro is a major public transportation project designed to improve mobility across the Saudi capital. The metro network connects important areas of Riyadh through multiple lines and stations, providing an alternative to private cars and helping improve public transportation accessibility. The project is an important part of Riyadh’s urban development because the city has experienced significant population growth and expansion. The metro is intended to reduce traffic congestion, improve connectivity, and support more sustainable transportation. Stations and supporting infrastructure are designed to integrate with other forms of public transportation, creating a broader network for residents and visitors. The Riyadh Metro also supports the wider transformation of the capital into a more connected and efficient city and contributes to Vision 2030 goals related to quality of life and urban development.

9. Saudi Data and AI Authority: Building a National AI Ecosystem

The Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority, known as SDAIA, was established to support Saudi Arabia’s development in data and artificial intelligence. SDAIA works on national strategies, digital initiatives, data governance, and artificial intelligence programs that support the country’s economic and technological transformation. The organization has contributed to initiatives designed to increase the use of data and AI across government and other sectors. SDAIA also supports national capabilities by promoting education, training, research, and innovation in artificial intelligence and data technologies. Its work is connected to Saudi Vision 2030 and the goal of developing a knowledge-based and technology-driven economy. The organization represents the technological side of Vision 2030 by focusing on digital transformation, AI capabilities, data infrastructure, and the development of Saudi talent.

10. Vision 2030 Quality of Life Program

The Quality of Life Program is one of the programs established under Saudi Vision 2030 to improve the everyday experiences and well-being of people living in Saudi Arabia. The program focuses on areas such as culture, entertainment, sports, tourism, urban environments, and community participation. It supports the development of new activities and facilities that provide residents and visitors with more opportunities for recreation and cultural engagement. The program also contributes to the development of cities and destinations that offer a wider range of lifestyle experiences. Examples of its areas of focus include expanding entertainment options, increasing participation in sports, supporting cultural activities, and improving public spaces. The Quality of Life Program demonstrates that Vision 2030 is not limited to economic development but also includes social and cultural transformation.
